# Research profile: Jonas Broe Bendtsen (PhD, ITA Oslo, Euclid Consortium)
# Edit freely: this text is given to Claude to score how relevant each paper is.

PhD project: "Constraining gravity and cosmological models using the Euclid survey".
Supervisor Hans A. Winther; collaborator Gábor Rácz. Member of the Euclid working group
"Simulations of models beyond ΛCDM". Goal: fast, accurate non-linear theory predictions
(approximate simulations + emulators) for models beyond ΛCDM, and tests of
relativistic / inhomogeneous cosmologies.

## Highest relevance (score 3 if the paper is centrally about these)
- Hybrid Lagrangian bias expansion / hybrid EFT (Kokron, Zennaro, Ibañez et al. 2024 style):
  basis fields (1, δ_L, δ_L², s², ∇²δ_L) advected with simulation particles, their 15
  auto/cross power spectra, hybrid-bias and displacement emulators, field-level bias models.
- COLA simulations (Winther et al. 2017, FML library), approximate/fast N-body methods,
  LPT/2LPT, scale-dependent growth in COLA, fixed-and-paired initial conditions.
- Modified gravity N-body and emulators: Hu–Sawicki f(R) (F4/F5/F6), DGP, JBD, symmetron,
  screening, μ(a)/G_eff parametrisations; Sesame-style power spectrum emulators (Mauland,
  Winther, Ruan 2024) and emulators for beyond-ΛCDM models in general.
- Backreaction and averaging in inhomogeneous cosmology: Buchert averaging, kinematical
  backreaction Ω_Q, emerging spatial curvature, silent universes, Simsilun, AvERA (Rácz et
  al.), timescape (Wiltshire), Räsänen; separate-universe approximations, volume counting.
- Exact GR inhomogeneous models: LTB / ΛLTB, Szekeres, large local voids (KBC void),
  observer effects, ray tracing / light cones in inhomogeneous universes.
- Relativistic N-body / relativistic 1+3 covariant approaches on top of simulations.

## Medium relevance (usually score 2)
- Euclid preparation papers on non-linear modelling, simulations of beyond-ΛCDM models,
  galaxy bias, galaxy clustering and weak lensing theory predictions.
- Dynamical dark energy / w0wa (DESI BAO DR1/DR2 evidence for evolving DE), quintessence,
  Hubble tension (local vs global H0), S_8 tension, "acceleration without Λ".
- Perturbation theory for LSS (EFTofLSS, LPT), non-linear power spectrum modelling,
  emulators / ML surrogates for cosmological observables, simulation-based inference.
- Simulation methodology: resolution/convergence studies, smoothing of initial fields,
  shell crossing, multistreaming, zoom simulations, non-periodic boundaries.
- Cosmological likelihoods combining Planck CMB, DESI BAO and SNe Ia (Pantheon+, Union3, DES Y5).

## Low relevance (score 1 at most)
- Pure CMB instrumentation, 21cm/reionisation astrophysics, galaxy formation details,
  inflation model building, primordial black holes, dark matter particle physics,
  gravitational waves, unless they connect directly to the topics above.
